# Nanded food, hotels, cafés, cinemas and markets research

Checked 2026-10-16. `food-hotels.json` contains **39 places**: 15 hotels/pilgrim stays, 13 restaurants/sweet shops, 6 cafés, 2 cinemas, and 3 shopping places. This is a curated expansion set, not an exhaustive census, a fame ranking, or a claim that every business has been contacted. `verifiedAt` means the cited public page was inspected/search-indexed on that date. Pages can be stale; operating hours, ratings, prices, personal contacts and promotional claims are intentionally omitted from game copy.

All records refer to Nanded-Waghala and its adjoining Vishnupuri/Wadi/Taroda urban area, Maharashtra. Search results for **Nanded City in Pune** were excluded. Exact coordinates are null unless obtained from a venue's explicit map pin. Area-based game positioning should be labelled approximate, never presented as a surveyed city model.
