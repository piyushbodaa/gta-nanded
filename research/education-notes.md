# Education research — 16 October 2026

`education.json` contains 45 institutions/branches: 13 coaching centres, 24 higher-education/technical campuses and 8 schools. 44 entries use first-party institution or government sources; Cambridge Vidyalaya is explicitly directory-sourced. This is a researched city/metro set, not a complete current census of every tuition class or school. Source access does not verify daily opening status. Some government affiliation records are older; their publication year should not be confused with the research access date.

## Coordinates and identities

- Latitude/longitude are left null unless a source or the existing independently researched local reference supplied them. Do not silently present interpolated area positions as exact street addresses.
- IIB Parnakuti, RCC, Konale NEET, Yeshwant and Pharmacy coordinates reuse the user's existing `counterstrike.nanded.fun/nanded-strike/public/landmark-reference.json` and `district-landmark-reference.json`. The IIB coordinate is an official directions-linked place pin. RCC is a researched frontage rather than the inset Google place pin. Konale is the official map point. Yeshwant is the official campus map centre, not a surveyed gate. Pharmacy is an official geotag.
- Aakash latitude/longitude are published on its official contact page.
- N.E.S. Science College and Science College are the same institution. People’s College is separate. Do not create a separate generic N.E.S. college pin.
- Yeshwant College of Physical Education in CIDCO is separate from Yeshwant Mahavidyalaya on VIP Road.
- SGGS engineering institute in Vishnupuri is not the similarly named Guru Govindsinghji journalism college listed in some older directories as CIDCO, with a contradictory Taroda/Malegaon Road address.
- Narayanrao Chavan Law College is verified opposite Government Polytechnic beside Yeshwant College; do not confuse it with the Pune college carrying a similar Chavan name.
- Chate lists three distinct Nanded branches at Purna Road, Stadium Road and Chaitnynagar/Taroda Budruk Road. Konale lists a Shyam Nagar NEET centre and a separate IIT/JEE centre at Shivaji Putala, Railway Station Road.

## Conflicts retained for review

Its lower prose table still says **Plot 3, Kamal Heights, Bhagya Nagar Main Road**, and its structured coordinate is `19.176008,77.3113287`, matching the older Kamal Heights directory point. Its foundation subpage's cached search result says Kamal Heights, but the live page also renders Ganesh Heights. The included entry uses the live heading but has null coordinates and an explicit localDetail caveat. Do not count two confirmed currently operating branches from this inconsistent record. The old Overture record additionally has incorrect Uttar Pradesh/postcode metadata; ignore that address metadata.

## Further coverage

The existing Counterstrike map has 102 education listings in a small city-centre extent, which may supplement this researched set as explicitly mapped/directory entries. Broad candidates include smaller individual subject tuition and commerce classes; their operational status and branch identity were not all separately verified here.

District-only entries such as JNV Shankar Nagar (Biloli), KV CRPF Mudkhed and colleges in Kandhar, Degloor, Bhokar or Mahur were excluded from this city/metro set. Search results often confuse “Nanded City” in Pune with Nanded in Marathwada; those Pune results were excluded. Kulkarni Science Academy search hits showed Nashik branches, so no franchise was guessed for Nanded.

Useful source hubs for continuing verification: [SRTM University affiliation section](https://srtmun.ac.in/academic-affiliation-section/), [Nanded district colleges](https://nanded.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/colleges/), [Nanded district schools](https://nanded.gov.in/en/public-utility-category/schools/), and individual institution contact pages stored with each entry.
